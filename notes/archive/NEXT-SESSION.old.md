## Pitfalls learned (read before starting)
- The verifier output only shows the *first* mismatch; use `MM4_TRIES=1`, `harness/slotlog.js <otrace> <ptrace> from to` to see what the game did per slot, and Lua exec/write callbacks (copy a kept `ptrace_r<seed>.lua`) for more. Mesen CPU address of bank-3E code `code_3EDxxx` is `$Dxxx`.
- Dispatch in `run_ai` is by pointer only (no bank): check for address collisions before adding an arm (stage-2/5 enemies live at `$9xxx`-`$BCxx` in banks `$3A/$3B`, bosses at `$A7F9`/`$B045`-`$B6xx` in `$35/$38`).
- A routine that sets its own `ptr` and has no RTS before the next label **falls through into the next state in the same frame** (enemy `13` needed this; `$942B` -> `$9470` too).
- The original's carry is **clear** for a hit in `code_3FF95D`; `ROL $0480,x` after it stores that carry as a scratch byte (the verifier compares scratch bytes!).
- Single-enemy ROMs keep a stale `$B300` cursor table: recordings that cross a checkpoint restart must use the original ROM (`mm4_orig.nes`).
- Per-section resets (`code_3ECEE1`) and the `$2F` camera direction matter for anything vertical; vertical sections with flag `$20` lose their direction flags after the entry scroll (`$2B &= $2F`).
- `TPCUR` for a single-enemy ROM is the index of the first entry with page >= p in that ROM's own list: `node harness/b300.js <rom> <stage> <pages>` (the ROM's `$B300` table is stale). `TPSECT=sect,startpage,$2B,$2D` in decimal; section byte `$B530[sect]`: low nibble = last screen index, high nibble = flags, `$2B = flags|sect`.
- Single-enemy recordings: first state of an enemy that spawned before the first row is NOT verified (checklist shows `ported`); also record from a spot before the enemy (teleport earlier) or on the original ROM so it spawns during the recording.
- Teleport y must be in open air/on floor: inside rock the game starts dead in hurt mode (mismatch at t=0 with state 7). Section 6/7-style `$20` vertical sections: after the entry scroll `$2B` loses its direction bits (stage 7 section 7: `$67` -> `$27`); use the `$2B` the game really has at that point (try both when a chain does not fire).
- Never reuse a seed number (it overwrites the kept trace). Keep the ROM and ripped assets private; never push the disassembly repo upstream.
